# Use the dual-flow home lockup as the canonical brand signature

The lockup in `docs/design/screens/in_cio_dual_flow_minimal/code.html` is the canonical Sombreado identity because it is the strongest accepted branding example. Its sun-and-shadow mark, literal charcoal and alabaster colors, Newsreader wordmark, geometry, and spacing should be reproduced faithfully; the bus-side split motif remains specific to product diagrams rather than serving as the logo.
